The March Madness Origin Story

"When I was a kid, my older brother Joe got invited to Brian Cugliari's March Madness tournament where he and his friends played in a driveway all day long. I thought it was the coolest thing and I wanted to do the same. So starting in sixth grade, my mom helped me make it happen. I invited my friends and my neighbors over to my house, ranked everyone by skill level, created teams that I thought were fair and made sure to mix personalities for best team chemistry results. Each team had to wear the color designated to them - often customized shirts were sported. We'd borrow a basketball hoop and wheel it down the street and up my driveway, chalk the court and had hotdogs, Becka chips and Gatorades. I did it from 6th grade to 12th and it was always one of my favorite days of the year. Playing basketball is one of my favorite things in the world and a bachelor party is usually an event where the bachelor gets to do whatever he wants - so I'm taking advantage of this opportunity to run back a childhood dream and do the March Madness tourny one last time with a little bigger of a budget and support. Appreciate you all!"
